# Doxa Privacy

**Privacy risk management for small teams.**

Doxa helps organizations without a privacy department understand and document their privacy compliance position.

## What Doxa does

- **Free privacy notice scanner.** Checks a live website privacy notice against ten standard disclosure elements and returns a Disclosure Coverage Score with the gaps listed in plain language. No account required.
- **Regulation-specific notices.** CCPA/CPRA, CalOPPA, GLBA (Reg P model form), HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices, and a general privacy notice. Self-serve, or reviewed by an IAPP-credentialed privacy professional.
- **Privacy Impact Assessments.** Data mapping, risk register, mitigation plan and executive summary, with an AI module where automated decision-making is in scope.
- **For agencies and practitioners.** Unlimited notices, bulk client management, white-label delivery and API access.

## Our approach: privacy in context

Doxa is built on **contextual integrity**, the framework developed by privacy scholar Helen Nissenbaum. Every information flow is described by five parameters:

| Parameter | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Subject | Whose information is it? |
| Attribute | What type of information? |
| Sender | Who shares it? |
| Recipient | Who receives it? |
| Purpose | Why is it shared? |

A privacy notice either discloses these clearly or leaves them ambiguous, and ambiguity is where enforcement actions start.
